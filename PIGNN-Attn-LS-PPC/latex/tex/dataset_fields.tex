%==============================================================================
\section{Motivation: Why Cache Grid-Only Fields?}
%==============================================================================

Each row of a power-flow Parquet dataset contains two fundamentally
different kinds of data:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Grid structure} — topology, admittances, bus types.
    These are \emph{identical} across all 36{,}000 scenarios in a single
    dataset file.
  \item \textbf{Per-scenario perturbations} — load/generation setpoints,
    initial voltages, Newton--Raphson solutions.
    These are \emph{unique} to every row.
\end{itemize}

For a large grid such as LVN Heo1 ($N=722$ buses, $n_l=796$ branches),
the dense admittance matrix $Y_{\rm bus}$ alone occupies $722^2 \times
8\,\mathrm{B} \approx 4.17\,\mathrm{MB}$ per row.  Storing one copy per
row would require $4.17\,\mathrm{MB} \times 36{,}000 = 150\,\mathrm{GB}$
of RAM --- almost entirely for data that never changes.

\texttt{Dataset\_optimized\_complex\_columns.py} avoids this by decoding
the grid-structure fields \emph{once}, storing a single shared copy, and
referencing it from every row.

\section{The 20 Cached Grid-Only Fields}
%==============================================================================

Up to 20 fields are identified as grid-only and cached at dataset
initialisation.  They are grouped by physical meaning below.

\subsection{Bus Features (3 fields)}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Field & dtype & Shape & Description \\
\midrule
\texttt{bus\_type}   & int64   & $(N,)$ & Bus classification: PQ=0, Slack=1, PV=2 \\
\texttt{vn\_kv}      & float32 & $(N,)$ & Rated bus voltage [kV]
                                           (e.g.\ 3, 20, 110, 380) \\
\texttt{V\_base\_bus} & float32 & $(N,)$ & Per-bus voltage base [V]
                                           $= \texttt{vn\_kv} \times 1000$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\texttt{V\_base\_bus} is derived from \texttt{vn\_kv} and is stored
separately because it is used repeatedly in per-unit conversions (see
the per-unit reference document).

\subsection{Bus Shunt (1 field)}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Field & dtype & Shape & Description \\
\midrule
\texttt{Y\_shunt\_bus} & complex64 & $(N,)$ &
  Per-bus shunt admittance (p.u.) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Shunt admittances (capacitor banks, reactors) are fixed grid equipment;
they do not vary with load/generation scenarios.

\begin{table}[h]

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsection{Branch Admittances (5 fields, per-unit)}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Field & dtype & Shape & Description \\
\midrule
\texttt{Branch\_y\_series\_from} & complex64 & $(n_l,)$ &
  Series admittance, from-bus self-term \\
\texttt{Branch\_y\_series\_to}   & complex64 & $(n_l,)$ &
  Series admittance, to-bus self-term \\
\texttt{Branch\_y\_series\_ft}   & complex64 & $(n_l,)$ &
  Series admittance, from-to mutual term \\
\texttt{Branch\_y\_shunt\_from}  & complex64 & $(n_l,)$ &
  Shunt admittance at from end \\
\texttt{Branch\_y\_shunt\_to}    & complex64 & $(n_l,)$ &
  Shunt admittance at to end \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

These five entries encode the $\pi$-model of each branch and are already
converted to per-unit using the appropriate bus-base scaling:
$\texttt{y\_series\_from} \times V_f^2/S_{\rm base}$,
$\texttt{y\_series\_to} \times V_t^2/S_{\rm base}$, and
$\texttt{y\_series\_ft} \times V_f V_t/S_{\rm base}$
(see the per-unit reference for the derivation).

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Field & dtype & Shape & Description \\
\midrule

The relevant code in \texttt{Dataset\_optimized\_complex\_columns.py}:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python]
grid_field_names = [

    "Branch_f_bus", "Branch_t_bus", "Branch_status",
    "Branch_tau", "Branch_shift_deg",
    "Branch_y_series_from", "Branch_y_series_to", "Branch_y_series_ft",
    "Branch_y_shunt_from", "Branch_y_shunt_to",
    "Is_trafo", "Branch_hv_is_f", "Branch_n",
    "Y_Lines", "Y_C_Lines",
]
if not self._no_cache_dense_ybus:
    grid_field_names.append("Ybus")
\end{lstlisting}

$Y_{\rm bus}$ accounts for $\sim\!98\%$ of the cached footprint.
Without grid-field sharing, loading all 36{,}000 rows would require
$4.25\,\mathrm{MB} \times 36{,}000 \approx 153\,\mathrm{GB}$, almost
entirely due to $Y_{\rm bus}$.  The shared cache reduces this to a
single 4.25\,MB copy.

\section{The 8 Per-Row (Non-Cached) Fields}
%==============================================================================

The following fields vary across scenarios and cannot be shared:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Per-row fields (perturbation-dependent, one copy per scenario).}
\label{tab:perrow}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Field & dtype & Shape & Description \\
\midrule
\texttt{U\_start}  & complex64 & $(N,)$    & Initial voltage phasor \\
\texttt{U\_newton} & complex64 & $(N,)$    & NR converged voltage phasor \\
\texttt{V\_start}  & float32   & $(N, 2)$  & $[|V|, \angle V]$ of initial voltage \\
\texttt{V\_newton} & float32   & $(N, 2)$  & $[|V|, \angle V]$ of NR solution \\
\texttt{S\_start}  & complex64 & $(N,)$    & Initial complex power injection \\

These represent the operating condition (load profile, generation dispatch)

\texttt{S\_start} provides the power setpoints $P^{\rm set}$, $Q^{\rm set}$
that enter the physics loss.

\section{Summary}
%==============================================================================

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{20 grid-only fields} (19 without \texttt{Ybus}) are decoded
    once at dataset init and shared across all rows.
  \item $Y_{\rm bus}$ dominates: it is $\sim\!98\%$ of the cached footprint
    (4.17\,MB out of 4.25\,MB for Heo1).
  \item Without sharing, 36k rows of Heo1 would require $\sim\!153\,\mathrm{GB}$
    of RAM; with sharing, only $\sim\!4.25\,\mathrm{MB}$ is needed for the
    grid structure.
  \item \textbf{8 per-row fields} hold the scenario-specific perturbations
    (voltages, power injections) and are stored individually.
  \item Setting \texttt{\_no\_cache\_dense\_ybus=True} drops \texttt{Ybus}
    from the cache; the model then reconstructs it on-the-fly from the
    branch admittance fields.
\end{itemize}
