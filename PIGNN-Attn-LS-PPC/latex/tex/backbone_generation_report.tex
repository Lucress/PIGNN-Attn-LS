\begin{abstract}
This report documents the Phase~0 backbone data-generation campaign for the
PIGNN-Attn-LS Newton--Raphson AC power-flow surrogate across 28~PPC benchmark
grids plus the ENTSO-E RealGridTest case.
The backbone preset applies $\pm40\%$ global load scaling, per-bus P/Q/gen
jitter, PV setpoint variation, and randomised initial voltages---but no
topology or admittance perturbation (fixed Y-bus, \texttt{share\_grid=True}).
Round~1 results are reported together with a diagnosis of failures and the
corrective Round~2 resubmission plan.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Backbone Preset Parameters}

All grids were generated with \texttt{--scenario\_level backbone}, which sets:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Value} \\
\midrule
Global load scale range & $[0.60,\; 1.40]$ \\
Scale generators with load & Yes \\
Per-bus load P jitter ($\sigma$) & 0.10 \\
Per-bus load Q jitter ($\sigma$) & 0.15 \\
Per-bus gen jitter ($\sigma$) & 0.05 \\
PV voltage setpoint range & $[0.95,\; 1.05]$\,pu \\
Random initial voltage & Yes \\
Initial angle jitter & $5^{\circ}$ \\
Initial magnitude jitter (PQ) & 0.02\,pu \\
Line outage probability & 0.0 (topology OFF) \\
Admittance jitter ($\sigma$) & 0.0 (Y-bus fixed) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Each grid targets 36{,}000 rows (19 chunks $\times$ 1{,}900 + 1 $\times$ 1{,}800
for array jobs; single batch for small grids).  Non-converged samples are
dropped via \texttt{--drop\_nonconverged}.  The Newton--Raphson solver uses
$K{=}40$ maximum iterations with mismatch-infinity convergence checking.

\subsection{Cluster Environment}

Jobs ran on the FAU Fritz HPC cluster (Intel Xeon, 72 cores per node),
partition \texttt{singlenode}, with 24-hour wall time.  Conda environment
\texttt{Python3.12\_PandaPower3.4.0}. Output parquets are stored on
the \texttt{/home/vault} filesystem.

\subsection{Failure Analysis}

Three distinct failure modes were observed:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{NODE\_FAIL} (case1354pegase, case1888rte, GBnetwork, partial
  chunks of case6470rte/case6495rte): Fritz compute nodes crashed during
  execution---an infrastructure issue unrelated to the code.  These grids
  produced 4-byte empty parquet placeholders.

\item \textbf{TIMEOUT} (case2848rte, case2869pegase, case3120sp): Generating
  36{,}000 samples on a single node for grids with 2{,}800--3{,}100 buses
  exceeds the 24-hour wall-time limit.  Each NR solve takes $\sim$2--3\,s per
  sample at this scale, giving $\sim$20--30\,h for the full 36k.

\item \textbf{Low convergence} (iceland~0\%, case\_illinois200~0.3\%,
  case33bw~1.9\%, case89pegase~4.3\%, case145~8.9\%, case300~42.3\%):
  The $\pm40\%$ global load-scaling range pushes these grids far beyond their
  NR convergence basin.  Contributing factors:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{case33bw}: Radial (tree) topology---any significant
      load perturbation causes voltage collapse on long feeders.
    \item \textbf{iceland}: Likely a weakly-meshed island grid with
      narrow voltage stability margins.
    \item \textbf{case89pegase / case145}: PEGASE-family grids model
      realistic European transmission with tight reactive-power limits.
    \item \textbf{case\_illinois200}: Known to have a narrow feasible
      operating region.
    \item \textbf{case300}: Partial success (42.3\%)---the upper load-scale
      region ($>1.2\times$) causes most divergences.
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l c l}
\toprule
\textbf{Grid} & \textbf{Buses} & \textbf{Round~1 Failure} \\
\midrule
case1354pegase & 1{,}354 & NODE\_FAIL \\
case1888rte    & 1{,}888 & NODE\_FAIL \\
case2848rte    & 2{,}848 & TIMEOUT \\
case2869pegase & 2{,}869 & TIMEOUT \\
case3120sp     & 3{,}120 & TIMEOUT \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Each array task generates $\sim$1{,}900 samples with 24 workers; total 36{,}000
per grid. An \texttt{afterok}-dependent merge job stitches chunks into the final
parquet.

\textbf{GBnetwork} (2{,}224 buses) is resubmitted as a single-node job---it
failed due to NODE\_FAIL (not timeout), so the original configuration is
retained.


\subsection{Low-Convergence Grids: Narrowed Load Range ($\pm20\%$)}

Six grids with convergence rates below 50\% are resubmitted with a narrower
global load-scaling range of $[0.80,\; 1.20]$ (down from $[0.60,\; 1.40]$):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l r r l}
\toprule

case\_illinois200 & 117     & 0.3\%  & $\pm20\%$ range \\

If $\pm20\%$ still produces unacceptable convergence rates, a further
narrowing to $\pm10\%$ will be applied for the most fragile grids
(case33bw, iceland, case\_illinois200).


\subsection{Partial Array Chunks: Manual Merge}

For case6470rte (11/19 completed) and case6495rte ($\geq$16/19 completed),
empty 4-byte chunk parquets from NODE\_FAIL tasks were deleted.
The available chunks can be merged into partial datasets ($\sim$21k and
$\sim$30k rows respectively) or the failed chunks resubmitted individually.

\section{Next Steps}
\label{sec:next}
% ===================================================================

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Monitor Round~2 completion.}
  Wait for all Round~2 jobs to finish, then audit convergence rates for the
  narrowed-range grids and row counts for the newly-arrayed medium-large grids.

\item \textbf{Per-grid convergence analysis.}
  After all data lands, compile a per-grid convergence-rate table.
  For grids still below $\sim$80\% convergence at $\pm20\%$, further narrow
  to $\pm10\%$ or consider dropping them from the Phase~0 benchmark set.

\item \textbf{Merge all partial array datasets.}
  Run the merge script for case6470rte, case6495rte, case6515rte,
  case9241pegase once their array tasks stabilise.  Decide whether to
  accept partial row counts or resubmit failed chunks.

\item \textbf{ENTSO-E RealGridTest.}
  The ENTSO-E array job is queued and uses \texttt{--label\_solver
  pandapower\_nr}. Monitor separately---this is the only non-PPC
  backbone grid in the campaign.

\item \textbf{Phase~0 training sweep.}
  Once all backbone parquets are complete, launch the Phase~0 training
  campaign across all grids with the PIGNN-Attn-LS model.
  Key metrics: RMSE on voltage magnitude and angle, physics-loss
  (power mismatch), and generalisation to unseen load scenarios.

\item \textbf{Phase~1 planning: Topology perturbation.}
  With Phase~0 baselines established, design the Phase~1 topology
  perturbation experiments:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Select small/medium grids ($\leq$300 buses) for
      \texttt{share\_grid=False} line-outage experiments.
    \item Use the ratio-mode contingency sampler (55/27/18 N-0/N-1/N-2 mix).
    \item Compare attention-head activation patterns between
      fixed-topology (Phase~0) and variable-topology (Phase~1) models.
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Summary}
% ===================================================================

Phase~0 Round~1 achieved full 36{,}000-row datasets for 13 of 24 single-node
grids.  Seven grids failed due to infrastructure issues (NODE\_FAIL, TIMEOUT)
and are being resubmitted as array jobs.  Six grids showed low NR convergence
at the $\pm40\%$ load range and are being regenerated with a $\pm20\%$ range.
Four large array jobs (case6515rte, case9241pegase, ENTSO-E, and partial
case6470rte/case6495rte) are still running or pending.

Once Round~2 completes, the Phase~0 backbone dataset is expected to cover all
28 PPC benchmark grids plus ENTSO-E RealGridTest, providing the training data
foundation for the PIGNN-Attn-LS Newton--Raphson surrogate.
